How to draw
  • Drag a corner to move it, or its inside handle to cut the corner back. With Snap to lanes on, corners snap to a lane line; Alt places them freely.
  • Add point (or A), then click the floor or the track to add a corner: after the selected corner, or into the track it's near. It stays on until you press it again, A or Esc; a plain click never adds a corner. Keyboard: with it on, N / P pick a corner and Enter adds one halfway to the next.
  • Delete, Backspace or right-click a corner to remove it.
  • Pick a jump, click the racing line to place it, drag it along. Pick Start line to drag the start line the same way.
  • Drag empty space to pan, wheel to zoom. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Ctrl+S save.
  • Errors and the full checks are on the Advanced tab.
