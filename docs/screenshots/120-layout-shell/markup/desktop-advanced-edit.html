How to draw
  • Drag a corner point (the small dot is the racing line; the diamond is the outside corner of the pipes). Snap: lanes against the walls and beside other lanes; Shift = 25 cm grid, Alt = free.
  • H on a corner: make it a hairpin (two points a lane apart).
  • Add point (or A), then click a straight to add a point there (or press and pull). It stays on until you press it again or Esc; a plain click never adds a point. Keyboard: with it on, N / P pick a corner and Enter adds a point halfway to the next one.
  • Delete or right-click a point to remove it.
  • Wheel over a point: corner radius · Ctrl + wheel: lane width · O: square / round outside corner.
  • Pick a jump, click the track to place it, drag it along. Drag the start line the same way.
  • Drag empty space to pan, wheel elsewhere to zoom. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo, Ctrl+S save.
