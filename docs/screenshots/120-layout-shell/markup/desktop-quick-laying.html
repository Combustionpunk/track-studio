1 · Lay the lanes

Press on a square and drag through the squares in driving order, straight or corner to corner for a diagonal. Drag back to take squares up.

To cross the lane (a figure of eight), go straight across it at right angles. Add a crossover there once the loop is closed.

Come back to the first square (the ring) to close the loop.

Keyboard: Tab to the plan's lane grid; the hint over the plan lists the keys.

Turns are 45°, 90° or 135°. Step 2 shapes them and shows where lanes squeeze or overlap.

Arrows move the cursor; Enter or Space starts the lane there. Then the arrows lay it a square at a time, Shift and two arrows in turn (say Up then Right) lay a diagonal, and Backspace takes back the last square.
Press on a square and drag to lay the lane, straight or diagonally. Turns are 45°, 90° or 135°. Step 2 shapes them and shows where lanes squeeze or overlap.
How to draw
  • Press on a square and drag through the squares in driving order: straight, or corner to corner for a diagonal.
  • Drag back over the squares to take them up.
  • To cross the lane, go straight across it at right angles.
  • Come back to the first square (the ring) to close the loop. Then you shape it.
  • Turns are 45°, 90° or 135°. Step 2 shapes them and shows where lanes squeeze or overlap.
  • Keyboard: Tab to the plan's lane grid; the hint over the plan lists the keys. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo.
