# Quick mode allows a track to cross itself

In Quick mode's step 2, a tap on the track while a corner is selected adds a corner after the selected one, as a floor tap does, even if the loop then crosses itself. Quick mode never refuses or "fixes" a crossing: clubs build tracks that cross over themselves, with a jump or bridge at the crossing. How the engine's checks treat a crossing is a separate question (#50). Accepted by the Board (Yeti, 2026-11-05) in #48.
